Your data on Deepresearch.se

A research project, not a product. DeepResearch.se is experimental — put into service solely to research which capabilities and use cases fit the Se/cure + Se/rver platform architecture. It is not here to answer real questions for real use; please don't rely on it for any real-world purpose.

Your messages are processed by Berget.ai (EU-hosted AI models) to generate answers. When web search is on, search queries derived from your question are sent to Exa to find sources — Exa may retain those queries under its own policy (see the documentation for a two-step workflow around this). You can change who does the searching: press and hold the web-search knob and pick this site's Worker, and the searches run from our own Cloudflare Worker instead, so no search company receives the query. Attached photos and documents are sent along with any embedded metadata they carry (e.g. a photo's location, or a Word document's author and edit history) — the attachment card shows a badge whenever that's the case, before you send. A photo's coordinates are also resolved to a place name via OpenStreetMap Nominatim (only the coordinates are sent, nothing else about you or your message) (see the documentation for details).

Your chat history and attached files rest in two places: this browser, and this site's Cloudflare storage, so your history follows your account across devices. On this signed-in tier that cloud copy is always kept — the tier is the choice, and there is no switch. Both copies are encrypted, with a key the server never writes down anywhere (see the History panel and the documentation for how that works); the readable exceptions are what's indexed for search — large documents, project files and notes, and chats inside a project (indexed so the project's other chats can draw on them) — together with the search index itself (indexing needs readable text). Deleting a conversation erases it in both places. Large attached documents are indexed with Berget's embedding model so questions retrieve only the relevant excerpts. To improve the service, the server logs completed exchanges — your question, the answer, and the research steps — unless you start the conversation with the ghost (incognito) toggle, which keeps it out of chat history and the server's log alike. If your connection drops mid-answer, the finished answer is held server-side for up to 15 minutes so your device can pick it up when it reconnects — it's deleted the moment it's delivered, or when the 15 minutes pass. Cookies: your login session and this notice's acknowledgement. Preferences (model, time target) stay in your browser.
