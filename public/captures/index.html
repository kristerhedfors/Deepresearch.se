Waiting to review

Recorded runs of the research pipeline — one agent, one model, one of the shipped example prompts — edited down for sharing. They are all here, one under the next: scroll the whole list and review any one you like, in any order. Swipe right (or →, or the 👍 button) to keep a clip, swipe left (or ←, or ✍️) to send it back. A left swipe opens a note field: feedback without words is a shrug, so the note is required and lands where the re-record loop reads it (scripts/captures --status needs_work).

A card stays where it is once you have filed it, wearing what you decided — so a swipe you did not mean is one tap to undo, and the clip goes straight back on the queue.

Every capture carries a number — #CAP-12 — and a short name, so you can ask for one by number. A clip that was sent back is re-recorded rather than overwritten: the newest cut plays by default and the older ones stay one tap away, each stamped with the commit it was recorded at.
